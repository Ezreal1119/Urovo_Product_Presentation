Sources & configuration notes

i9600 Mini Smart Mobile Terminal · non-financial configuration · source checks: November 2, 2026

Platform and source edition

The controlling source is the user-designated i9600 Mini Smart Mobile Terminal (EN)-SPEC-20260908 DOCX. Its table lists octa-core A75 2.0 GHz, Android 15 with GMS, 3 GB RAM + 32 GB ROM and a 6.745-inch 720×1600 display. No performance benchmark, future OS upgrade commitment or multi-app performance guarantee is inferred. The separate Smart MiniPOS specification is not used.

Barcode and camera configuration

The rear camera is 5 MP AF as standard and 16 MP AF as an option. The professional scan engine is optional. There is no front camera as standard; 2 MP FF and 16 MP AF front cameras are optional. Camera code capture follows the supplied customer copy and matching September Smart Mobile specification. Test the selected camera/app or scan engine on the actual labels; no decode-speed or accuracy result is claimed.

NFC and non-financial positioning

The original DOCX lists NFC. The matching September Smart Mobile specification describes built-in under-display NFC. The slide calls out built-in NFC without asserting payment certification or an exact antenna boundary. This source does not establish EMVCo, PCI or a supported SoftPOS payment solution for this configuration. Membership, tag identification and lookup scenarios require compatible tags and business software. No on-device bank-card acceptance is advertised.

Battery and charging

The DOCX specifies a removable 3.85 V / 5,000 mAh lithium-polymer battery and a 5 V / 2 A adaptor. Nominal energy is 19.25 Wh (calculated as voltage × capacity). Removable does not mean hot-swappable: save work and follow the operating procedure before a battery exchange. The optional four-pin magnetic pogo interface and optional magnetic charging cable are listed separately. No charging-time, full-shift runtime or uninterrupted battery-swap claim is made.

Networks, location and card slots

Standard communications: FDD-LTE, TDD-LTE, WCDMA, GSM; Bluetooth 5.0; IEEE 802.11 a/b/g/n/ac at 2.4 and 5 GHz. Positioning lists GPS, A-GPS, BeiDou, GLONASS and Galileo; no positioning accuracy is asserted. Default slots: one Micro SIM + one Nano SIM + two SAM + one MicroSD. Optional slot configuration replaces the Micro SIM with eSIM while retaining Nano SIM, two SAM and MicroSD. Application, carrier and regional configuration compatibility must be checked.

Physical and environmental specification

177 × 79.8 × 17 mm; 298 g; power button, volume +/- and custom key. Operating temperature 0–50°C; storage -20–70°C; 5–95% RH non-condensing. The source has no IP rating or drop rating. No weatherproof, industrial ruggedness or vehicle-mount compatibility is implied. The hand strap is standard and no onboard printer is specified. Printed receipts are an external workflow, not an included printer accessory.

Accessories and interfaces

Standard accessory: hand strap. Optional accessories: Kensington security lock, screen protector and magnetic charging cable. Standard interfaces: USB Type-C USB 2.0 OTG and five-pin pogo. Optional interface: four-pin magnetic charging pogo. No charging dock, multi-slot cradle, scan handle, spare-battery sales kit or mounting accessory is added to the supplied accessory list. Prepare charging spares only where supplied for the project.

Official competitor sources and comparison scope

SUNMI L3 official Chinese datasheet, page 2: SUNMI OS 4.0 based on Android 14; 6.75-inch 720×1600 display; 4/64 GB or 6/128 GB; 175×79×15 mm; 282 g; IP68; 1.5 m concrete drop; -20–55°C operation. Its default card layout is one Nano SIM + MicroSD or two Nano SIM, with optional PSAM and eSIM. Its battery is 4.4 V / 5,000 mAh and hot-swap is optional in this sheet. Dual-sided NFC is qualified to the non-RFID version. The official website describes EMVCo PCD L1, SoftPOS, fingerprint and optional UHF capabilities. These are L3 strengths, not i9600 Mini features. The visible row compares OS and card-slot layouts; it does not assert that L3 lacks GMS.

iMin Swift 1 Pro official product page: Android 13, GMS, 4/32 GB, 6.5-inch 720×1600 display, optional NFC, 4G, Wi-Fi ac and Bluetooth 5.0; base PDA weight 245 g; IP54 and 1 m drop. The page lists 168.3×79.3×17.2 mm; the official modular brochure lists a different base-body size, 77×162×11 mm, and optional scanner/printer modules. Dimensions are omitted from the comparison to avoid mixing revisions or configurations. Weight is for the base PDA; a scanner module adds weight. Both sources specify a 7.6 V / 2,500 mAh / 19 Wh battery, so the 5,000 mAh i9600 Mini is not presented as double the energy or runtime. GMS is shared. No matched price, TCO, CPU performance or certification superiority is claimed.

The visible comparison lists i9600 Mini configurations in the middle column and the named competitor configurations in the right column. It is not an exhaustive specification matrix. Public sources and configurations can change; confirm the regional SKU before quoting.
