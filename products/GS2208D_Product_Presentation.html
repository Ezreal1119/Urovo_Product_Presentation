Product identity and controlling source

Resolution and speed

GS-2208D: 8 dots/mm (203 dpi), direct thermal, maximum print speed 203.2 mm/s (8 ips), maximum print width 54 mm. Speed is a rated print maximum, not measured cups-per-minute or a guarantee for every barcode, driver, label size or network condition. The six emulations are EPL, EPL 2, ZPL, ZPL II, DPL and TSPL; this is a command list, not a claim of six universally interchangeable systems.

Thermal labels and cup applications

Direct thermal printing uses compatible thermal media without a ribbon. The supplied PDF lists monochrome as standard, a dual-colour version as an option requiring dedicated dual-colour thermal paper, and a 127 mm maximum label roll outside diameter. It also lists wristband, retail and restaurant applications. This presentation focuses on coffee and small-format order labels; it does not claim medical certification, food-contact approval, waterproof output, cold-cup condensation resistance, adhesive suitability or linerless/cutter capability.

Cup, ice, sugar and pickup data are produced by the connected ordering app and label template. The illustration's drink data is generic. Test adhesive/media performance on the actual hot/cold cup or packaging surface. QR or barcode printing does not itself establish scanning accuracy or an operational pickup system.

Physical specification and revision differences

The supplied PDF lists 129 mm (W) × 165.3 mm (H) × 244.3 mm (D), 1.3 kg, ABS enclosure. Its 165.3 mm height controls over the customer AI draft's 163.5 mm. The manufacturer's current product page instead lists 1.35 kg; the visible metric retains the supplied edition's 1.3 kg. The PDF specifies operation 5–40°C and 25–85% RH non-condensing; storage -40–60°C, 10–90% RH. These are not weatherproofing or splash-resistance claims.

The PDF lists FCC, CE, CCC, BIS and CB and RoHS/REACH/WEEE compliance. No regional certification is advertised as freshly verified for every configuration.

Supplied interfaces and configuration scope

The supplied PDF lists USB 2.0 high-speed 480 Mbps and internal Ethernet 10/100 Mbps, with optional internal Bluetooth 4.0 and Wi-Fi. The current manufacturer page lists Bluetooth 4.2 while older manuals include economic/empower variants and USB Host. The visible slides say Bluetooth generically and do not promise USB Host on the supplied configuration.

GAINSCHA GS-2208D official product page. Confirm delivered interfaces and regional availability when specifying a unit.

Formats and integration

The supplied PDF lists EPL, EPL 2, ZPL, ZPL II, DPL and TSPL compatibility; Windows printer utility, Windows SDK and Android SDK; BarTender Windows drivers and label design software available via download. Firmware emulation is not a guarantee that every template, font, barcode or host application works unchanged. No iOS SDK, automatic app connection or current software licensing entitlement is inferred.

Included items and optional equipment

Included: Windows labeling-software CD, quick start guide, USB cable, power cord and external switching power supply. Power: input AC 100–240 V; output DC 24 V / 2.5 A / 60 W. Dealer option: peeler. User option: external roll mount. Internal Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are configuration options. Dual-colour printing is an optional version requiring matching dual-colour thermal stock, not a software-only toggle or standard capability. The source does not list a cutter or supplied label roll.

Two-inch desktop alternatives

TSC DH220 official product family page and manufacturer datasheet: DH220 is direct thermal, 203 dpi, maximum 8 ips / 203 mm/s, 54 mm print width, and 127 mm roll outside diameter. These match the principal GS-2208D figures and are not presented as a speed advantage for GS-2208D. Other DH variants are not substituted silently.

Zebra official ZD411 technical specification: 56 mm print width at 203 dpi, USB and USB Host, with Ethernet available as an option. The comparison selects the direct-thermal ZD411D configuration. Zebra's web manual and product/specification materials have differing speed wording, so no Zebra speed comparison is printed. Options, languages, management tools and application compatibility must be matched to the delivered SKU. ZD510-HC is omitted because its cartridge wristband focus is less direct for the coffee-label task.

The visible table describes the reference models and GS-2208D separately. No price, lifecycle, TCO, clinical certification, universal barcode superiority or drop-in replacement advantage is inferred.
