[1] K329: thermal labels and receipts

The original K329 specification PDF (two pages, image-based) was rendered and read directly. It states 120 mm/s, 203 dpi and thermal line printing. The dedicated brochure explicitly supports receipts and labels. Actual speed and barcode readability depend on media, density, label content and the host application. Test the customer template. Two-color and watermark printing are marked optional in the original specification and are deliberately omitted from the main page.

[2] K329: Bluetooth and passive NFC pairing

The dedicated specification lists Bluetooth 4.2 standard and 5.0 optional, with passive NFC tag pairing and optional 2.4/5 GHz WLAN. NFC here identifies the printer for a compatible host to initiate pairing; it is not a payment reader or proof of NFC payment acceptance. Validate host OS/app support. The dated price-book Bluetooth shorthand differs, so the dedicated specification takes priority. WLAN is not advertised as standard on K329.

[3] K329: daily handling protection

The original PDF page 1 explicitly lists IP54 and a 1.5 m drop rating. IP54 denotes dust protection and protection from splashing water under test conditions; it does not establish immersion resistance or unlimited outdoor exposure. Neither a longer product life nor a specific warranty is inferred. The memory positions K329 in retail and courier work; its informal replacement-life and price commentary is not a customer-facing lifespan or price promise.

[4] K389: 2 m drop testing and carrying weight

The May 8, 2026 dedicated specification lists 390 g including battery, excluding paper, and 2 m drops onto tile over concrete over −10 °C to 50 °C, with MIL-STD-810H referenced. The slide uses “2 m drop-tested” rather than extending that test to every accident. No K389 IP rating is established by the reviewed specification; K329 IP54 is not inherited. Weight is not compared to an unspecified configuration or competitor.

[5] K389: replaceable 3200 mAh battery and hot swap

The dedicated specification page 3 lists a user-replaceable 7.2 V, 3200 mAh / 23.04 Wh pack and “Hot swap: Support”. This supports power continuity during battery exchange, using a compatible charged spare and the prescribed procedure. It does not guarantee preservation of every print job or application session. Runtime depends on usage; the presentation does not promise a 12-hour shift or reproduce “double runtime” without a defined baseline. Spares and chargers should be confirmed in the quotation.

[6] K389: dual-band Wi-Fi and 802.11r

The specification lists Wi-Fi 5 on 2.4/5 GHz and IEEE 802.11r fast transition. 802.11r reduces authentication work when moving between correctly configured compatible access points; infrastructure, security settings and firmware must support the deployment. It is not a guarantee of zero lost packets or uninterrupted print queues. Bluetooth and USB-C are also supported. These printers receive print data from host applications; no bundled WMS/MES or scanner is implied.

[7] Applications and workflow benefits

The user-designated memory mem_6c75655fc423131f places K329 in retail and outdoor/courier work and K389 in warehouse/manufacturing. The K329 brochure supports store labeling/receipts and courier use; the K389 introduction supports warehouse receiving, put-away, picking and manufacturing labels. Price/return, stock and batch/serial workflows on this page are applications of those capabilities, not named customer case studies.
